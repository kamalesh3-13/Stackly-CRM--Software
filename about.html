Our Story

From one spreadsheet to a full success team.

2018

One spreadsheet, one success manager

Marcus started account management out of a shared spreadsheet, tracking deals by hand for eleven clients he called every week.

2020

Remote account management, same standard

We took the weekly check-in model online — the same close account management, minus the requirement to be in the same city.

2022

Reporting lab opens

Added win-rate modeling and structured forecasting, so "pipeline health" became a number we could actually show you, not a feeling.

2025

640+ clients, nine success managers

Still capped client-per-manager ratios on purpose. We'd rather turn people away than let account management quality slide.

Why We Cap Ratios

We turn people away on purpose.

Every success manager at Stackly caps how many clients they'll take on. It means we say no more often than we'd like — and it means the clients we do take on get seen, every single week.

No success manager carries more clients than they can actually watch closely A waitlist exists so quality doesn't slide when demand spikes Every check-in gets a real reply — never a queue of unread messages
